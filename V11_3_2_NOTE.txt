V11.3.2 - FIX SOL TAB DISCOVERY + HONEST ACTION STATUS

Root cause in V11.3/V11.3.1:
The SOL scraper looked for li[role="tab"]. The SOL Blazor page renders group tabs
as <li> children of .rz-tabview-nav without role="tab". The scraper therefore
found zero group tabs, retained the original partial data and (in the previous
main()) returned exit code 0. That made Actions appear successful without
updating Milano.

Fixes:
- Discover groups from .rz-tabview-nav > li (fallback selectors included).
- Click and verify the active group tab from the selected Radzen class.
- Open the Calendario button and parse its rows.
- Require all expected competition names and group tabs before replacing the
  SOL catalogue; otherwise fail the Actions job and retain the last good data.
- Do not return success when the SOL sync failed.
- Attach SOL logs and debug HTML/screenshots when the job fails.
- Open the admin fixture picker on Milano-Monza-Lecco, with an unselected
  competition placeholder so all 18 competition choices are visibly available.
